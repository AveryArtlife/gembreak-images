The story

Supposed to be gone in March.

Cha Cha, 4, a fawn pug from Coweta County, Georgia. Everything below is from the family's account as reported by WSB-TV, People, Parade and Complex. The clinic refers questions to its lawyer.

March 2026

Goodbye

Senoia Animal Hospital says Cha Cha has organ failure and diabetic ketoacidosis. The family pays for euthanasia and says goodbye.

Months later

A Facebook photo

Rebecca Belote sees Cha Cha, alive, in a photo. She is living with a clinic employee.

The confirmation

"She's healthy"

A former clinic worker confirms it. The employee's husband confirms it, and says the dog is in good health.

The cover-up

Put down for real

The family asks questions. According to them, the clinic takes Cha Cha back and euthanizes her. A healthy dog.

Now

The complaint

Complaint filed with the Georgia State Board of Veterinary Medicine. The county solicitor is looking. No charges so far. A second family says the clinic did the same with their cat, Annie.

"The fact that they put a healthy dog down. That's just sick."Rebecca Belote, Cha Cha's owner, to WSB-TV

FAQ

Fair questions.

Are you affiliated with Cha Cha's family or the clinic?

No. $CHA is a community tribute. We are not connected to Rebecca Belote, her family, or Senoia Animal Hospital. If the family ever asks us to change anything, we will.

Is there a team allocation?

No. 100% of supply enters through the public bonding curve. The launch wallet bought 1.5–2% in the open market at creation, is published, and is locked for 30 days.

What is the Cha Cha Pledge, exactly?

pump.fun pays token creators a share of trading fees. 50% of every claim goes to the public Pledge wallet, and from there to named animal-welfare organisations. Candidates: Best Friends Animal Society, ASPCA, Coweta County Humane Society. Every transfer is posted with its hash.

Will you harass the clinic?

No, and neither should you. Nobody's name, face or address gets posted here. Anger goes to the Georgia State Board of Veterinary Medicine complaint, which is where it can actually do something.

Why "Forever"?

Because the story shouldn't end when the news cycle does. And because Cha Cha is also a dance, and she dances here.

Is this financial advice?

No. $CHA is a memecoin with no utility and no expectation of financial return. Only spend what you can afford to lose.
