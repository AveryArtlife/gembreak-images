# 04 · Launch plan: $CHA

**Recommended launch:** Thursday **Oct 30, 2026, 10:30am ET**, or Tuesday Nov 3, 10:30am ET if the socials/site are not ready. The story broke Oct 26–28; after ~Nov 5 the window is mostly closed unless a new catalyst lands (vet board ruling, lawsuit, national TV).
