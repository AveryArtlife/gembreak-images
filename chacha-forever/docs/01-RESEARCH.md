## 1. The asset we are building on: the Cha Cha story

| Fact | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Cha Cha, a 4-year-old fawn pug owned by Rebecca Belote and her family (husband, 17-year-old son) of Coweta County, Georgia | WSB-TV (Oct 26, 2026) |
| What the family was told | In **March 2026** Senoia Animal Hospital said Cha Cha had organ failure and diabetic ketoacidosis and recommended euthanasia. The family paid for it and said goodbye. | WSB-TV, Parade, Yahoo/People |
| The discovery | Months later Belote saw Cha Cha alive in a Facebook photo. A former clinic worker confirmed an employee had taken the dog home. The employee's husband confirmed it too, saying the dog was healthy. | WSB-TV, Parade |
| What happened next | After the family pressed for answers, the clinic allegedly took Cha Cha back and euthanized her. "The fact that they put a healthy dog down. That's just sick." | WSB-TV, The Mary Sue |
| Clinic response | Refers all questions to its lawyer. In a *separate* case (Annie the cat, kept alive 18 months after a paid euthanasia) it said it "fell short in honoring the trust the family placed in us." | WSB-TV, Yahoo |
| Official status | Complaint filed with the Georgia State Board of Veterinary Medicine; the county solicitor is looking at it; Fox 5 reported it may not meet the threshold for a crime. | Yahoo, Fox 5 via search |
| Reach so far | Complex (11M IG followers) posted it Oct 28: ~12.7K likes, 472 comments. Covered by People/Yahoo, Parade, WSB-TV, Fox 5, LADbible, Unilad, AOL UK, The Mary Sue. | Instagram embed, search |

**Why this is a memecoin-grade story:** it has every ingredient Peanut the Squirrel had. A named, photogenic, innocent animal. A faceless institution as the villain. A betrayal twist (alive, then killed to cover it up). An unresolved justice arc that keeps generating headlines (vet board, solicitor, possible lawsuit, the second victim Annie). And it is **two days old**.

Pronoun note: WSB-TV uses "she/her"; Complex used "him". Brand copy uses **"she"** (the local, original report) or simply "Cha Cha".

## 2. Comparables: what "animal justice" coins actually did

| Coin | Trigger | Launch → first peak | Peak | What drove it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **$PNUT** Peanut the Squirrel | NY wildlife officials seized and euthanized a pet squirrel (Oct 30–Nov 1, 2024) | Launched Nov 1–2 on pump.fun → **$130M in 3 days** | **~$2.4B** on Nov 14 after Binance spot listing (Nov 11) | Elon Musk posts, election-week outrage, "Justice for Peanut" framing, rapid CEX ladder (LBank Nov 3 → Binance Nov 11) |
| **$MOODENG** Moo Deng | Viral baby pygmy hippo at a Thai zoo (Sept 2024) | ~2 weeks to ~$640–700M | ~$700M; doubled again on Binance Futures listing | Pure cuteness + relentless meme supply; no villain needed |
| **$CHILLGUY** | A single illustrated character meme (Nov 2024) | $10M → $500M in ~a week | ~$500M | Character licensing drama became free press |
| **Justice-for-Cha-Cha tokens** (6+ launches, Oct 27–29, 2026) | This story | Never left the bonding curve | < $10K live; one $165K print with zero liquidity (dead) | No brand, no coordination, no amplifier. The name "Justice for Cha Cha" is now burned. |

**Takeaways**
1. The outrage-plus-cute combination (PNUT) outperformed cute-alone (MOODENG) by roughly 3x at peak.
2. The first 72 hours decide whether a coin becomes *the* coin for a story. Six copycats have already failed; the narrative is still unclaimed because none of them looked like a real project.
3. CEX listings are step functions, not the origin of the pump. They follow trending status, holder count, and social volume.

Sources: Crypto Briefing ($PNUT $1B after Binance), iTiger/CoinDesk syndication (PNUT $130M in 3 days), LBank Labs 2024 review (PNUT dates), Cryptonomist and Crypto Briefing (MOODENG Binance Futures), CoinMarketCap Academy (CHILLGUY).

## 3. What the base rates say

- **79% of pump.fun tokens trade only on their launch day** (Bitquery data cited by crypto.news, Oct 2026).
- Industry estimates of 98%+ of pump.fun launches failing or rugging (Medium/Coinmonks 2026 outlook).
- The coins that survive the first day share: a recognisable face, a story people repeat without being paid, a visible team, and a reason to keep posting after the first pump.

Implication: our edge is not "launching first", it is **launching finished**. Brand, site, memes, socials, KOL list and the first-hour script all exist before the contract does.

## 8. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| News cycle dies in 5–7 days | High | Launch now; pivot the content engine from "justice" to "forever" (memorial wall, pet-tribute UGC) by day 4. |
| Family publicly rejects the coin | Medium | Respectful framing, no family photos, no claims of affiliation, a visible pledge, and a polite outreach *after* launch offering the fund with no strings. Never use their name in the token name. |
| Copycats and snipers at launch | Certain | Finished brand, CA released in three places simultaneously, modest creation buy, no CA pre-sharing, Bubblemaps-clean wallet behaviour. |
| "Charity coin" scepticism | Medium | Public wallet, monthly on-chain report, fees routed automatically where possible. Promise little, show receipts. |
| Platform fee/mechanic changes | Low | Re-verify fee tiers and graduation threshold day-of. |
| X/TG spam-reports from rival launches | Medium | Verified-style profile completeness, no mass-DM, no engagement farming bots. |
| Legal | Low–Medium | Memecoin disclaimers; no promises of returns; no harassment of named clinic staff; direct anger to the vet board complaint link. |
