# 02 · Brand: Cha Cha Forever ($CHA)

## The name

**Token name:** Cha Cha Forever
**Ticker:** $CHA
**Short form:** CHA CHA
**Tagline:** *Justice for Cha Cha. Forever on-chain.*

### Why "Cha Cha Forever" beats the alternatives

| Option | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Cha Cha Forever** | ✅ chosen | Unique in the launch field (six "Justice for Cha Cha" tokens already launched and died). Works after the news cycle ends: "forever" is a memorial, a dance (*cha cha forever*), and a holder's mantra ("never selling"). Clean to say, search and merch. |
| Justice for Cha Cha | ❌ | Burned name (6+ dead launches). Also locks the brand into victimhood; nothing to post once justice is served or stalls. Keep it as the *tagline*, not the name. |
| CHACHA4VR | ❌ | Reads as "VR", unpronounceable in a tweet, un-searchable, looks like a 2021 BSC token. |
| Cha Cha the Pug | ❌ | Descriptive, not evocative; competes with pet accounts. |
| Cha Cha the Immortal Dog | ❌ | Already launched (x3), and "immortal" is too close to the cruel twist. |

The tagline keeps the justice energy; the name keeps the brand alive. Headlines write themselves: *"Cha Cha Forever: the pug a vet clinic couldn't bury twice."*

## One-liner and elevator pitch

**One-liner:** $CHA is the memecoin for Cha Cha, the Georgia pug a vet clinic was paid to put down, secretly kept alive, then killed when the family found out.

**Elevator pitch (30s):** In March a Georgia family paid Senoia Animal Hospital to euthanize their sick pug, Cha Cha. Months later they found her alive on an employee's Facebook. When they asked questions, the clinic took her back and put her down. No charges. Cha Cha Forever puts her on the one ledger nobody can quietly edit, and sends half of every creator fee to animal welfare. Justice for Cha Cha. Forever on-chain.

## Voice: three registers, one character

| Register | When | Sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| **Heart** | Story posts, memorial wall, press | Plain, short, no hashtags inside sentences. "They told her family she was gone. She wasn't." |
| **Justice** | Reply-guying news posts, calls to action | Firm, factual, points to official channels. Never names clinic staff personally. "Ask the Georgia vet board what happened to Cha Cha." |
| **Fun** | Memes, TikTok, TG stickers, price posts | The dance. "Cha Cha real smooth." "Slide to the left (of the chart)." "Everybody clap your paws." |

**Do**
- Lead with Cha Cha's name, not the ticker.
- Use the halo. It is the brand's one symbol.
- Credit reporters (WSB-TV's Tom Jones broke it). Journalists share things that credit them.
- Say "pledge" and show the wallet. Never say "charity token".

**Don't**
- Never claim affiliation with the Belote family or Senoia Animal Hospital.
- Never use the family's real photos of Cha Cha. All art is original (see `prompts/`).
- Never post clinic employees' names, faces or addresses, or encourage anyone to. Point to the vet board complaint instead.
- Never promise price, listings, or returns.
- No sad-dog art. Cha Cha in our world is radiant, dancing, haloed. The sadness is in the story, not the character.

## The character: Cha Cha

- Fawn pug, black mask and ears, big round eyes, tongue-out half the time.
- **A gold halo**, always slightly tilted (she's a pug, it slipped).
- One ear flopped forward. Head tilt 10°.
- She dances. Default pose: mid cha-cha, one paw out.
- Accessories (sparingly): a tiny gavel, a gold "$CHA" tag on her collar, clouds.
- Never: tears, a vet table, a syringe, a cage, a gravestone.
