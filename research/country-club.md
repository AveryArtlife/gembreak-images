## Killed this round (so you stop guessing)

- **Senior-living and hospice roll-ups already strip-mined the heraldic/estate lexicon:**
  Ensign (NASDAQ skilled nursing), Pennant (home health/hospice), Laurels (34 nursing homes),
  Chancellor (senior living), Oakmont (118 luxury senior communities), Colonnade, Signet
  (largest US behavioral-health mgmt co). Any hit there makes the name read "nursing home".
- **Tenure** — thematically perfect, but Tenure Health is a live retiree preventive-care
  startup. Dead.
- **Health-owned lexicon:** Diadem (Amgen-backed biotech), Paddock (Perrigo generics),
  Regatta (two health cos), Portico (a compounding + HRT pharmacy — your exact lane),
  Veranda (medical group offering weight loss — your exact lane), Lyceum (specialty-pharma
  SaaS), Lodestar, Polestar (Volvo EV), Consul, Emissary (three health cos), Bulwark,
  Provenance (Provenance Meals, wellness), Clubhouse (the app; says the quiet part out loud).
- **Towns fail on a fixed pattern** — every affluent town already has "[Town] Medical Group"
  plus an independent compounding pharmacy: Sagaponack (Sagaponack Medical PC — exact),
  Wellesley (Wellesley Medical Group is a medical weight-loss clinic — a direct GLP-1
  competitor), Darien (three), Hillsborough, Kenilworth (Merck's campus), Telluride (Kia),
  Hobe, Chevy Chase, Montecito ($6.5B healthcare REIT + "Trust" restriction), Nantucket.
- **Famous clubs and funds — don't.** A pharmacy called Maidstone/Sebonack/Baupost/Third
  Point Rx is free-riding on goodwill that dilution law exists to protect; Augusta National
  is the model (sues resellers, auction houses and squatters). **Shinnecock is a hard legal
  no** — *In re Shinnecock Smoke Shop* (Fed. Cir.) upheld a §2(a) false-connection refusal
  because the Shinnecock Nation is an "institution". **Piping Rock is a hard no** — Piping
  Rock Health Products is a ~$294M supplement manufacturer. Also burned: Oakmont (senior
  living), Greylock (a clinic), Bessemer (a pharmacy), King Street (an apothecary), Carlyle,
  Bridgewater, Maidstone (six pharmacies), Medinah, Pine Valley, Cypress Point. Geography-only
  survivors were Stanwich (Tier 2) and, weakly, Lone Pine / Silver Point / Farallon (fund
  associations dominate — not recommended).
- **Latin virtue-words are dead:** Praesidium, Praedium, Tutamen, Firmitas, Custodia,
  Dominium, Mansio, Vigilia — all owned by funds, banks, manufacturers or hotels.
- **Medical-negative surnames:** Whitlow (finger infection), Osgood (Osgood-Schlatter),
  Alderson (a pharmacy and a federal prison), Tremaine (Davis Wright Tremaine + a clinic),
  Ellsmere (three clinics under the standard spelling), Grandison (healthcare staffing).
- **Finance-owned roots:** Ashmere (Ashmere LP), Alderbridge (AlderBridge Wealth, Boca),
  Marshpoint (four firms), Ternpoint, Beacongate (a Cobalt Strike malware term), Weirbridge.
- **Wellness-owned:** Wyndmere (Wyndmere Naturals), Kingsleigh / Kingsley & Rowe (Kingsley
  Clinic telehealth), Ravensleigh (NHS psych unit), Harrowpoint (Harrow Health/ImprimisRx —
  compounding, fresh enforcement record).
- **Generator estate compounds are open because they're weak** — "-point/-crest/-mere" read
  subdivision; puns (Hartcove, Stagwater) and faux titles (Earlspoint, Dukespoint) fail. Only
  names that could sit on an Ordnance Survey map (Fairmere, Larkcroft, Alderhurst, Whitecombe)
  passed. The 741 bare-open compounds are in the CSV (segment `club`) for completeness.
- **"Trust" and "Club" are not free suffixes** — state banking-name rules and charitable
  register for Trust; Norwegian sports clubs and Halden prison for Club. "Reserve", "House"
  and "& Co" carry the exclusivity without the baggage.
