# Science, Research & Lab Domains — Biotech-Forward Naming

**Updated:** August 15, 2026 · Companion to `best-names.md`

A science/technology angle for the brand — names that read like a **research company or biotech**, not a consumer supplement. Generated by pairing peptide/amino/protein stems (and clean modifiers) with science suffixes (`sciences · research · labs · bio · biotech · biosciences · therapeutics · technologies · genomics · diagnostics · discovery · synthesis · proteomics`). **174 of 528 candidates are available on `.com`.**

> Two structural cautions specific to this theme:
> 1. **This is where the real biotechs live** — several generated names collide with actual companies (see the avoid-list). A science name invites confusion with clinical-stage firms, so the USPTO knockout matters even more here.
> 2. **"Peptide Sciences" itself is gone** (the shut-down grey-market vendor). Descriptive `Peptide + [science word]` names are clear to register but weak as trademarks — the coined names below are stronger.

## ⚠️ Avoid — these collide with real biotech companies
Even though several are "available," don't use them — confusion + trademark exposure:

- **Kindred Bioscience** → *Kindred Biosciences* (real biopharma, acquired by Elanco)
- **Ridge Bioscience / Ridge Sciences** → *Ridge Biotechnologies*
- **Onyx Bioscience** → *Onyx Pharmaceuticals* (acquired by Amgen)
- **Quanta Bioscience** → *QuantaBio* (life-science reagents)
- **Sable Bioscience** → *Sable Therapeutics*
- **Molecular Dynamics** → long-established scientific term / former company
