The names that survived

The shortlist was run through a hostile trademark, collision, and linguistics screen — every name searched for a reason to reject. Most failed. These are the survivors, each with its one remaining caveat, verified available across .com · .ai · .io · .gg · .club.

Two hard truths from the screen:
  1. "[Word] Peptide" is structurally weak — the second word is generic and unprotectable, and the space is full of "research use only" grey-market vendors, so regal / imperial / royal styling reads as a research-chem storefront.
  2. None of these are 100% clean — each is least-fatal, not cleared. Run a formal USPTO knockout (Class 5 / 31 / 44) and a live handle check before you build. This is a screen, not legal clearance.
