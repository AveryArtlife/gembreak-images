## The constraint that shapes every name

Synthetic peptides such as BPC-157 and TB-500 **are not lawful dietary-supplement ingredients** — DSHEA
excludes synthetic molecules developed as drugs. April 2026 removed BPC-157 from FDA Category 2 and a July
2026 advisory committee voted 8–6 to recommend it for the 503A bulks list, but that is a *compounding*
pathway, not a supplement pathway. A brand selling peptides as supplements under a famous cofounder is the
most enforceable target in the category. Three lanes:

1. **Lawful peptide-adjacent supplements** — amino acids, collagen peptides, creatine.
2. **Prescription compounded peptides** via a LegitScript-certified telehealth.
3. **Research-use-only** — where the celebrity makes you a target rather than a brand.

The Top 100 is tiered so lanes 1 and 2 are separable, and so no name traps the business in lane 3.

**Most useful finding:** **Amino** is the only category noun here that is unambiguously lawful on a
supplement label. **Formula** and **Protocol** describe a blend or a programme rather than a molecule.
**Peptide** carries the search volume and the legal exposure in equal measure — it belongs on a product
line, not necessarily on the master brand.

## 8 · Method and limits

17,026 candidate domains were resolved at DNS in the 2 September 2026 sweep; NXDOMAIN treated as an open
lead. 4,751 came back open on `.com`. **This session could not re-resolve them** — outbound DNS,
RDAP and WHOIS are blocked by the network policy in this environment — so every domain here carries a
2 September date and **must be re-checked at a registrar before purchase**. Three names from the August
sweep (peptura, peptorra, peptidyne) were registered by others between sweeps, so assume a few percent of
the list has moved.

This is a naming and collision screen, **not legal clearance**. Before committing: USPTO knockout search in
Class 5 (supplements) and Class 44 (telehealth), social-handle check, and counsel on which lane the product
sits in. Collision notes come from web search on 18 September 2026 and catch live brands, not pending
applications.
