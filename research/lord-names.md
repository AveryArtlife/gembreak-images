## Honest caveats

- **"Available" = DNS-open lead** (no NS delegation). A registered-but-undelegated domain
  looks identical — confirm at a registrar before buying (the MalibuPeptide lesson).
- "Lord" brands carry a tone risk: they read gaming/meme-culture ("Gains Lord") more than
  medical. Great for a personality-led biohacking brand; wrong for the RUO/clinical lane.
- Trademark note: LOTR-adjacent phrasing ("Lord of the …") is fine generically, but avoid
  any Tolkien trade dress. Run the usual USPTO knockout (Cl. 5/44) before building.
