## 1. Competitive landscape (do NOT copy these)

Full annotated list lives in **`competitive-landscape.md`**. Summary of who owns the space:

### Human peptides — the naming is saturated
- **`[Modifier] Peptides`** is roughly half the research-vendor market: Core, Biotech, Polaris, Patriot, American, Royal, Koi, Ascension, Everest, Paramount, Prime, Iron Peak, Blue Sky, Vellora, Biogenix.
- **`Peptide [noun]`**: Peptide Sciences (shut down Mar 2026), Peptide Partners, Peptide Store, Peptide Plugs, Peptide Supply Co, Peptide Crafters.
- **Trust-signal adjectives as the brand**: Verified Peptides, Licensed Peptides, Protide Health — a fast-growing 2025–26 wave.
- **Amino- / -Labs / -Labz / Bio-**: Amino Asylum (FDA-raided), Amino Vault, Umbrella Labs, Behemoth Labz, Modern Bio, Cernum Biosciences. Grey-market vendors lean on offshore TLDs (`.is`, `.co`, `.store`, `.partners`, `.us`) because the `.com` is contested.
- **Telehealth deliberately avoids the word "peptide"**: Hims, Ro, Mochi, Henry, Eden, Found, Marek, Hone, Ivim, Defy, AgelessRx — short friendly names with `join-/try-/start-` prefixes.
- **Skincare** uses peptide-as-hero at the *product* level (HydroPeptide, Peptide 21, Multi-Peptide) but rarely at the company level (OneSkin trademarks a proprietary peptide, OS-01, as the moat instead).
- **Manufacturers** use compressed science compounds: GenScript, Bachem, PolyPeptide, AmbioPharm, CordenPharma, LifeTein, AnaSpec.

### Dogs / pets — "-tides", "paw-", and "pet-" are already crowded
- **`-tides` blends are taken in the exact niche we want**: **Pettides**, **Pawtides**, and Integrative Peptides' **PetTides** line. (This is why `puptides.com` is gone — see §5.)
- **`paw-`** is the single most overused morpheme: Pawtides, Pawfy, Paw Origins, HardyPaw, Wonder Paws, Honest Paws.
- **`pet-`**: PetLab Co, PetHonesty, Pet Matrx, Pettides.
- **`vet-`** for clinical authority: Vetster, VetriScience, Vetnique, Airvet.
- **Dog-longevity incumbents to respect**: Loyal (loyalfordogs.com), Leap Years, Rejuvenate Bio, MYOS Pet, plus collagen-for-dogs brands K9 Collagen, Pure Majesty, Pat's Pet Kitchen.

**Strategic takeaway:** the `[word] Peptides` / `Peptide [word]` / `Amino [word]` / `[word] Labs` patterns are saturated *and* increasingly associated with grey-market vendors and FDA enforcement. The open lanes are (a) a **coined single word** (Peptovi, Peptura), (b) an **unexpected concrete modifier** (Foundry, Cobalt, Kindred, Sentinel), or (c) for dogs, a **fresh pun that isn't paw-/pet-/-tides** (Pupcell, Pupitide, Collapup).

## 3. Availability method

Every candidate `.com` was checked against the **authoritative Verisign `.com` gTLD nameservers** (a/b/c/d/e/f.gtld-servers.net) with a direct `NS` query:

- **rcode 3 (NXDOMAIN)** → the name has no delegation → **AVAILABLE to register**.
- **rcode 0 (NOERROR, delegation present)** → **TAKEN**.

This is the same signal registrars use, and it is *not* fooled by parked/for-sale pages (those are registered → correctly reported TAKEN). Results in `domain_availability.csv`.

**Caveat:** near the end of the sweep the session's outbound DNS was rate-limited, leaving **26 names unverified** (Appendix A). They are *excluded* from the confirmed list below — recheck them at your registrar before buying.

**892 candidates checked → 147 confirmed available, 719 taken, 26 unverified.**
