## 1. Executive summary

Crypto is in a **liquidity- and policy-driven recovery rally off a bear-market bottom** — not a fresh all-time-high breakout. BTC bottomed at **$58,711 on June 30, 2026** (down ~53% from the October 2025 ATH of $125,072) and has ripped ~22% in one week to **~$76,650**, briefly testing $80K. The move was ignited by four stacked catalysts in a single week:

1. **Treasury liquidity:** Treasury Secretary Bessent announced a **doubling of long-term bond buybacks** (Aug 20), suppressing yields and reviving the "$40T debt debasement" trade.
2. **Regulatory momentum:** Trump convened a White House crypto summit and publicly pushed the **CLARITY Act**; the **SEC proposed "Regulation Crypto Assets"** (Aug 18); the CFTC promised crypto rules regardless of the bill and announced a path to bring **Hyperliquid perps onshore**.
3. **A historic short squeeze:** **$2.7B in shorts liquidated** (record since 2021), including $1B+ of BTC shorts in about an hour.
4. **Real flows:** **$2.6B into spot BTC+ETH ETFs** in the week ending Aug 21 — the strongest week since October 2025 ($1.9B BTC / $697M ETH, five straight green days).

**The core tension:** social euphoria (BTC social dominance at a 52-week high, "altseason" mentions at 52-week highs) and record squeeze mechanics are colliding with a **hawkish Fed** — markets price essentially **zero chance of a September cut and a meaningful chance (~30–65% depending on source) of a 25bp hike** on Sep 16, with July CPI at 3.4%. This rally is fighting the Fed and leaning on Treasury liquidity and regulation as substitutes.

**Posture:** Constructive but disciplined. Buy dips, don't chase strength; keep dry powder for the **Sep 15–16 catalyst cluster** (CLARITY cloture vote Sep 15 + FOMC Sep 16); no leverage while funding sits near 19-month highs.

**Point estimates (probability-weighted):** BTC ~**$79–81K in 30 days**, ~**$84–86K in 60 days**, with a fat left tail (bear case $62–68K) if the Fed hikes and CLARITY stalls.

## 7. Portfolio strategy (risk capital only)

**Model allocation:**

| Sleeve | Weight | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Core | 55% | BTC 35%, ETH 20% |
| Large alts | 27.5% | SOL 10%, HYPE 10% (on pullback), XRP 7.5% (on retrace) |
| Narrative momentum | 7.5% | ZEC 5% (retrace-only), PUMP/ENA ≤2.5% |
| Dry powder (stables) | 10% | Deployed only into the bear scenario ($64–70K BTC) |

**Execution rules:**
1. **Staged entries:** ⅓ now (BTC/ETH/SOL), ⅓ on dips into the entry bands above, ⅓ only after Sep 15–16 resolves.
2. **No chasing:** every alt above has a defined entry band; if it runs without you, the answer is "next trade," not market-buy.
3. **No leverage.** Funding at 19-month highs means both directions liquidate violently ($2.7B last week).
4. **Take-profit ladders:** sell 25% of each alt position at each base-case target band; let the rest ride toward bull targets with trailing stops.
5. **Event risk:** into Sep 15–16, trim any position up >30% from entry back to original size.
6. **Thesis invalidation:** BTC weekly close < $64K → cut all alt positions to half, stop adding, reassess. Alts bleed 2–3x BTC's drawdown in a failed recovery.
7. **Sizing sanity check:** assume any single alt can −50% in a week; no position sized so that hurts more than ~3% of total portfolio.

**What would make us more bullish:** CLARITY passing cloture, Fed hold + neutral presser, ETF inflows sustaining >$500M/wk, BTC daily close >$80.5K, stablecoin inflows accelerating (currently thin — the main missing ingredient).

**What would make us bearish:** Sep 16 hike, CLARITY cloture failure, ETF flows flipping negative, funding >0.03% with price stalling, BTC losing $70K.
