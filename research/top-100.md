## The shape of what survived

- **Two-surname firms** (Rutledge & Ashby, Marlowe & Holt, Halloran & Vane, Winslow & Dane)
  are the only naming grammar with a near-zero collision rate in health.
- **Law and heraldry** (Chancery, Ermine, Exchequer, Sable, Coronet, Thistle) survive because
  no clinic wants to sound like a courtroom — which is the brief.
- **Invented English estates** (Fallowden, Tarnbrook, Stanmere, Fairmere, Larkcroft,
  Alderhurst, Brynpoint, Whitecombe) survive when they could plausibly be on an Ordnance
  Survey map and carry no pun or faux title.
- **One Latin standard-word** survived out of ~40 tried: Criterion. Every virtue/metal/truth
  word (Aurum, Argent, Veritas, Fidelis, Praesidium, Firmitas, Custodia …) is owned.
- **Suffix hierarchy held across all screens:** Rx > Clinic > Medical > House / Reserve >
  Therapeutics > Pharma > Medica > Science > Apothecary > Longevity > Meds / Wellness /
  Vitality / Formula. "Trust" and "Club" are penalized (state naming rules; prison/sports
  collisions); "Longevity" as a suffix lands you next to supplement and skincare operators.
