Rebuilt after three corrections: rank each domain on its own merit rather than grouping by lexical

two-word forms only.

## The availability limit, stated plainly

`halolab.com` and `mitoboost.com` both answer **NXDOMAIN** at public DNS today, yet both are registered.
A domain registered without nameservers — parked without DNS, held by an investor, or sitting in a
registrar's inventory — is invisible to a DNS check. **DNS proves a domain is taken; it cannot prove one
is free.** RDAP, WHOIS on port 43 and every registrar API are blocked by this environment's network
policy (re-tested today). So this list removes every name DNS proves is gone and ranks the rest by
strength; the confidence column grades how much to trust each NXDOMAIN, and short names score lowest
because those are the ones people quietly hold.

## What the live re-check changed

- **123 of 4,751** names in the old pool are now delegated — registered — a **2.6% loss in 17 days**,
  including `lucentpeptide.com`, which was in the v8 Top 100.
- **The single-word .com game is over.** 2,589 freshly generated short coined `.com`s were tested:
  **2,079 already delegated**, and the remainder were unpronounceable residue. Of 323 real English words
  on `.com`, three were DNS-clear, all reading as held assets.
- **Alt TLDs:** `.ai` 12 of 323 clear, `.io` 17, **`.club` 105** — `.club` is the only place single real
  words survive, and membership framing suits a subscription brand (Pep Club launched on that premise in
  July 2026 — validation and a collision to screen).

## Scoring

| Criterion | Weight | High score |
|---|---|---|
| Brevity | 25% | ≤6 chars full marks; ~1.5 points per 2 chars past 8 |
| Brandability | 25% | One word > two; 2 syllables > 4; penalties for mechanical prefixes, radio-test failures (ryze, thryv, vyv), consonant pile-ups |
| Meaning | 20% | Real semantic payload — vitality, recovery, clarity, strength, growth |
| Category fit | 15% | Rewards lawful supplement nouns (amino, formula, protocol); penalises grey-market suffixes and fat-loss verbs |
| Trademark strength | 15% | Arbitrary/coined > descriptive; live-brand collisions marked down hard |
