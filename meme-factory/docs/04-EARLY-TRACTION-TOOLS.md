# 04 · What actually gets a coin noticed in the first hours (and what is market making vs. a felony)

Ranked by effect-per-dollar in hour one, then hour two, then day one. Prices are Oct 2026 vendor figures.

## Hour 0–1: things that create the first chart

| # | Tool | What it does | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A finished kit at the moment of launch** | Site, character, socials, pins, thread, proof links all live when the CA drops | Internal | The only thing none of the 15 Cha Cha copycats had. Converts the first 200 curious clicks into holders. |
| 2 | **Creation buy (2–4%) from a public wallet** | Owns the bottom of the curve honestly; denies snipers the free float | 2–4 SOL | Published before the CA. Never bundled. |
| 3 | **Simultaneous CA drop** (X pin edit, TG pin, site) | Removes the "which CA is real" window copycats exploit | 0 | Script it; humans are 90 seconds slow. |
| 4 | **pump.fun livestream (Pump Live)** | Front-page presence on the venue where the first buyers already are; a face or a character on screen is the strongest trust signal in the first 30 minutes | 0 | The stream meta repeatedly produced the biggest 2025–26 pump.fun runners. Script the first 20 minutes. |
| 5 | **Reply-guying the story's top posts** | Puts the CA and site under the 3–5 posts (Complex, People, WSB-TV) where the audience already is | 0 | Short, respectful, one link. Five accounts max; more looks like spam and gets the thread filtered. |
| 6 | **Telegram raid coordination** (own group, raid bot) | Concentrates 50–500 holders' replies on target posts at set minutes | 0 (bot) | Organic members only. Paid "raid services" are bot farms; skip. |
| 7 | **DexScreener Enhanced Token Info** | Logo, socials, description on the page every terminal links to; ~15 min processing | $299 | Submit at T+2 min. Without it the token is a bare ticker. |
| 8 | **Graduation sprint** | Getting off the curve inside hour one is the first visibility step-function (PumpSwap pair → indexed on DexScreener/Jupiter/every terminal) | Community | Set the whole first hour's content around it. |

## Market making: the line

| Activity | Legal? | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Providing two-sided liquidity on DEX** (Meteora DLMM bins around the price, Memecoin Pool with permanently locked liquidity, Raydium CLMM) | Yes | Stage 3. Treasury deposits SOL + tokens, positions published, liquidity locked. Earns fees. Reduces the "thin book" that makes memecoins gap. |
| **CEX market-making agreement** with a known firm (two-sided quotes, spread/uptime targets, often a token loan with a call option at a strike) | Yes, with a real contract | Stage 4. Read the loan/option terms: the MM's upside is buying your tokens cheap at the strike; negotiate the strike and the loan size, and never accept "volume targets" in writing. |
| **Trading competitions** with disclosed prizes, **LP incentives** | Yes | Legal volume incentives. |
| **Volume bots, wash trading, "boost volume" services, self-trading across wallets** | **No.** Market manipulation; DOJ *Operation Token Mirrors* convictions in 2025 (Gotbit: founder sentenced, $23M forfeited; CLS Global: barred from US markets) and new charges in March 2026. | Never. Also: every terminal's "volume quality" tab now flags it. |
| **Bundled block-0 buying from hidden wallets** | Not per se illegal to buy; selling it into induced buyers is fraud exposure | Never. |

## The honest ranking for a $5K hour-one budget

1. Creation buy 3 SOL (~$500)
2. ETI $299
3. Livestream + raid + reply-guy (free, people)
4. Two micro KOLs after graduation ($600)
5. Pledge receipt (free, it is your own fee)
6. Hold the rest for Stage 2; a boost pack ($300–$1K) only if holders are climbing at hour 2.

What you do **not** buy in hour one: macro KOLs, boosts, "trending services", volume.
