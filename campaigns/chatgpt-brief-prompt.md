# Prompt: generate the pack-rip creator brief

Paste into ChatGPT with search enabled. Fill in the rate and turnaround under
Step 2 §8 first, and add any house style for the reveal (logo, code overlay,
end card) so the beat sheet accounts for it.

The verification rules in Step 1 are load-bearing — Instagram is largely
login-walled, and the default failure mode is confidently invented Reel URLs.

---

You are a UGC creative strategist. Produce a ONE-PAGE brief I can send to paid
UGC creators who are making **pack rip** and **pack rip reaction** videos for
GemBreak. One page means roughly 500–650 words — scannable, no preamble, no
filler.

## About GemBreak
GemBreak sells sealed mystery packs with real watches inside. Over 1,700 watches
in the current pool, from everyday pieces up to a Richard Mille RM 11-03 (~$330K),
which is the rarest pull site-wide. Packs are priced off the expected value of
what's in them. Creators open a pack on camera and keep whatever they pull.

## STEP 1 — Research before you write
Search Instagram, and the open web for indexed Instagram Reels, for current
high-performing examples of: watch and jewelry pack rips/breaks, mystery box
unboxings, trading-card breaks, and reaction-to-pull videos.

Find 5–8 real examples. For each give me:
- @handle and a direct link
- Rough performance if visible (views/likes)
- The ONE structural thing that makes it work — hook timing, camera angle,
  reveal pacing, where the reaction sits in the frame

Rules for this step:
- Only include links you actually retrieved and can confirm resolve. If you
  can't verify one, label it "UNVERIFIED" or drop it. Do NOT construct
  plausible-looking Instagram URLs.
- Instagram is largely login-walled. If you can only surface a few, say so
  plainly and give me the hashtags and search queries I should run manually
  instead of padding the list.

## STEP 2 — Write the brief
Sections, in this order:
1. **The two formats** — one line each on pack rip vs. pack rip reaction, and
   how they differ in what the viewer is watching for.
2. **Shot spec** — orientation, resolution, lighting, audio, where the pack sits
   in frame, whether hands/face are in shot, what to do about wind and street
   noise if shooting outdoors.
3. **First 3 seconds** — three hook options, written as actual lines the creator
   can say or put on screen.
4. **Beat sheet** — timestamped structure for a 30–45s rip. Where the tension
   is, how long the reveal holds, where the video ends.
5. **What makes a reaction land** — how to shoot genuine reaction without
   performing it, and how to frame someone else's reaction if a second person
   is in the video.
6. **Say this / don't say this** — two-column table.
7. **Banned words** — see Step 3.
8. **Disclosure and delivery** — #ad placement, raw + edited files, turnaround.

## STEP 3 — Banned keyword list
Build this from actual platform policy. Meta and TikTok both restrict gambling
and gambling-adjacent promotion, and mystery packs sit close enough to that line
that the wrong word gets a video suppressed or an ad account flagged.

Start from this seed list and expand it:
gamble, gambling, bet, betting, wager, odds, jackpot, casino, lottery, raffle,
sweepstakes, win money, cash out, payout, guaranteed, risk-free, can't lose,
easy money, get rich, investment, ROI, returns, flip for profit, double your
money

Also cover:
- Luxury brand names (Rolex, Patek, AP, Richard Mille) in anything that will run
  as paid media — trademark complaint risk even when the watch is authentic
- Any claim about what a pack is "worth" or what you're "expected" to get
- Anything implying the buyer profits

For each banned term give a one-word reason (policy / trademark / claim), and
give the safe substitute where one exists.

## Format
- Markdown. Headers and short bullets. Written to the creator in second person.
- Bold the non-negotiables.
- Put the Step 1 research examples in an appendix BELOW the one-page brief —
  they don't count toward the page.
- State any assumption you had to make instead of asking me first.
