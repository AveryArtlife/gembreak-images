# GemBreak × UGCTank — Street Interview Campaign

Two identical listings, one variable: who's posting. Variant A goes up as
**The Baddest Agency**, Variant B as **GemBreak** direct. Everything else —
rate, deliverables, timeline, creative brief, cities — is held constant so the
only thing the response data can be measuring is the identity on the listing.

before publishing.

### Rate card

Three knobs to set before posting — these are the numbers that move application
volume more than anything else in the listing.

| Knob | Recommended | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Base per video | **$250** | Market rate for sub-50k street-interview UGC with usage rights. Below $150 and applications skew to creators who won't actually shoot. |
| Performance bonus | **$150 @ 100k · $500 @ 500k · $1,500 @ 1M** | The bonus ladder is what makes a viral-hungry creator pick this over a flat-fee gig. |
| Affiliate | **12% of pack revenue, 30-day cookie** | Optional add-on. Include it in both variants or neither. |

Seeding cost per creator is one pack at COGS, not retail — budget it at pack EV,
not at the `EV × 1.10` sticker.

## Concepts to hand the creator

Pick one per creator; don't let them all shoot the same thing. Ranked by how
well the format travels.

1. **"Would you trade?"** — Creator offers a sealed pack to a stranger in
   exchange for the watch on their wrist. The whole video is the decision. Best
   format we have: real stakes, a visible clock, and the stranger does the
   emotional work.
2. **"Guess it, keep it"** — Strangers guess the value of the watch just pulled.
   Closest guess walks away with it. Costs one watch, buys a genuinely shareable
   payoff.
3. **"Beat my pull"** — Creator opens a pack, then walks the block comparing the
   pull to what people are wearing. Natural fit for the Design District and 47th
   Street, where the wrists are worth filming.
4. **"Appraise this"** — Creator takes the pull to dealers and asks them to price
   it cold. Works best in NYC; the dealers are already on camera-friendly turf.

**Do not brief:** anything built around odds, payout percentage, or "is it worth
it" math. See below.

## Compliance — read before writing any caption

These are creative constraints, not legal boilerplate. They change the copy.

- **Disclosure.** `#ad` in the caption *and* a spoken or on-screen disclosure in
  the first three seconds. Creator-side and brand-side both.
- **Language.** TikTok restricts gambling promotion, and the mystery-pack format
  sits close enough to that line to get videos suppressed. Brief creators to say
  *pack, pull, open, collect, trade*. Never *bet, gamble, odds, wager, jackpot,
  win money*. This is why concept 5 (walking strangers through the odds ladder)
  isn't on the list above — it's a good video that will get throttled.
- **No value claims.** Nobody says or implies a pack is +EV for the buyer. The
  `EV × 1.10` pricing means it isn't, and a creator saying otherwise is a claim
  we'd own.
- **Strangers on camera.** Anyone whose face is in a video used as a paid ad
  needs a signed release. Give creators a one-page release to carry — this is
  the single most common reason street-interview UGC can't be run as an ad.
- **18+.** No minors on camera, no targeting under 18 in whitelisted spend.

## Running the test

**Design:** Post both variants simultaneously, both cities, identical offer.
Simultaneous beats staggering by a week — a stagger lets platform seasonality
and UGCTank's own feed ordering contaminate the result far worse than the small
risk of a creator seeing both listings.

**Hold constant:** rate, bonus ladder, deliverables, turnaround, city targeting,
creator size band, application questions.

**Vary only:** the posting identity, the brand name in the copy, the profile and
bio, and the sender on any outbound DM.

**Measure over 7 days:**

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Applications | Raw top-of-funnel |
| **Qualified applications** | ≤50k followers, real street-interview history, right city. This is the number that decides it. |
| Time to first application | How fast each identity gets picked out of the feed |
| DM reply rate | Agency and brand outreach usually diverge more here than on the listing itself |
| Rate pushback | Agencies get negotiated against harder — worth knowing before you scale |
| Applied to both | Not noise. Tells you the listings are reaching the same pool. |

**Decision rule:** compare qualified applications, not raw. If the two land
within ~30% of each other, go brand-direct — same result, no agency layer, and
the brand equity compounds. Only run through The Baddest Agency if it wins
clearly.

**On reading the result honestly:** a niche listing like this pulls somewhere in
the range of 10–60 applicants. At that size, a 12-vs-9 split is noise. Don't
call a winner on a gap smaller than about 2×; if it's close, that *is* the
finding, and brand-direct wins on cost.

## Sourcing

The listing is inbound. Outbound is `scripts/find-ugc-creators.mjs`, which pulls
street-interview creators in both cities under the follower cap who posted in the
last 7 days, ranked by viral reach and tagged by category — watch, jewelry,
gambling, mystery pack, finance, crypto. Run it, then DM the top of each city
list from whichever identity that city is testing.
