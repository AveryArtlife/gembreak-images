# Outbound DMs — matched pair

Same offer, same length, same ask. The only difference is who's sending. Send
each city's list from one identity only — mixing them inside a city ruins the read.

Keep the first message under 60 words. Sub-50k creators get pitched constantly
and the long ones don't get read.

---

> Hey — we cast street-interview creators for brand campaigns and your [SPECIFIC

>
> Want the full brief?

**Follow-up (72h, once):**

> Following up on the watch campaign — still have slots for [CITY]. Happy to send

## Rules

- `[SPECIFIC VIDEO]` gets replaced every time. A creator can tell instantly
  whether you watched their stuff, and it's most of the reply-rate difference.
- One follow-up, then stop.
- Don't name the rate ladder in the first message. It reads as a bulk send.
- Log every send in `applicant-tracker.csv` with the variant, or the test
  produces nothing usable.
