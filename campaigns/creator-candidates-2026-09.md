## What this source could not give us

The spec ranks on comment rate first and cost-per-1k-views third. LunarCrush
reports a single aggregate "engagements" figure that does not decompose into
views and comments, so **neither of those is computable here**. What follows is
ordered by format fit, tier and recency instead — which is a weaker signal than
the spec calls for, and the reason the Apify run still matters.

Also not screened: engagement anomalies, giveaway farms, ad saturation, and
audience geography. US status is inferred from language and caption cues except
where noted as confirmed.

Coverage is **TikTok only**. LunarCrush's index is topic-based and built around
crypto, finance and major brands; "unboxing", "sneakers" and "jewelry" are not
tracked topics, and Instagram/YouTube returned nothing usable for these queries.
Everything below surfaced through the `pokemon` and `rolex` topics.

Window: posted on or after 2026-08-04.
